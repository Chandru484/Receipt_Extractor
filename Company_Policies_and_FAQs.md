# Tekis Enterprise & Store Policies, FAQs and User Guidelines

## 1. Return, Replacement and Refund Policy
- **Return Window**: Customers can return or exchange eligible purchased items within 15 calendar days from the date of the original invoice.
- **Proof of Purchase**: The original invoice or a verified digital receipt copy generated by the Universal AI Chatbot is strictly required for all returns and exchanges.
- **Condition of Goods**: Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all manufacturer tags, seals, and barcodes intact.
- **Refund Processing**: Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original mode of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Bank Transfer) within 5 to 7 business days.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Customized stationery items, opened chemical or paint containers, and promotional clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## 2. Product Warranty and Appliance Service Guidelines
- **Standard Warranty**: All electrical equipment and home appliances (including Crompton Ceiling Fans, exhaust fans, and LED fixtures purchased from LIMRAS Electricals) carry a 12-month (1-year) standard manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.
- **Warranty Claim Procedure**: To claim warranty service or request on-site repair, provide the invoice number (e.g. `INV-2026-001`) and serial number to the customer care team or upload the receipt in the chatbot.
- **Exclusions**: Damage caused by improper electrical voltage surges, physical drops, water submersion, or unauthorized third-party repairs is not covered under warranty.

## 3. GST, Tax Invoices & B2B Billing
- **GST Compliance**: All transactions generate a standardized GST invoice showing the CGST, SGST, or IGST breakdown along with HSN/SAC codes.
- **B2B Input Tax Credit**: Corporate clients providing their valid GSTIN at the time of purchase can claim 100% Input Tax Credit (ITC). To update your GSTIN or business address, contact billing support.
- **Digital Receipt Copies**: Customers can request, view, and download PDF copies of their historical invoices directly through the chatbot by asking "Download PDF receipt" or "Show my receipts".
